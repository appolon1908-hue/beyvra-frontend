## Event migration plan

NATS and JetStream identifiers are deliberately unchanged. A future migration must introduce a versioned compatibility bridge, attach shadow consumers without business effects, compare sequence/event IDs, then migrate idempotent consumers individually. Retirement requires `LOST_EVENTS=0`, `DUPLICATE_BUSINESS_EFFECTS=0`, and `ORDERING_VIOLATIONS=0` across soak and replay tests. No in-place stream or subject rename is permitted.

## Current guarantees

Public identity is Beyvra and legacy names remain invisible. Browser upgrades preserve preferences and sessions. New headers are preferred, old headers remain accepted, outbound webhooks emit both, compatibility routes retain Deprecation/Sunset/Link metadata, and metrics/loggers dual-emit. Financial Service, real-money state, production, database structures, NATS/JetStream, and infrastructure identifiers are unchanged.
